SUBJECT FILE

ŠPAČEK, PAVEL · PRAGUE, CZ

Twenty-five years. Hundreds of films. One location at a time.

I've been doing this work since 2001. The range has been wide — Czech and Slovak features, Hollywood studio pictures, Bollywood musicals, German, Turkish and Japanese productions. Each one had its own logic, and I've learned something from every single one.

I'm not chasing the biggest names. I'm interested in doing good work on projects that deserve it — whether that's a modest local film or a major international production. I bring the same care regardless of scale.

If you're looking for locations across Czech Republic or Slovakia, I can put together a curated selection from twenty-five years of scouting archive — or we can simply start with a conversation.

FIELD OPERATIONS

STANDARD PROCEDURE — 4 PHASES
PHASE 01SCT

Location scouting

Script breakdown, creative consultation, location selection. I read the material carefully and look for places that actually fit the story.

PHASE 02PRM

Permits & authority liaison

Permits, contracts, municipalities, police, monument care. I handle the paperwork so the production doesn't learn it the hard way.

PHASE 03MGT

On-set location management

Base camp, set protection, logistics, clean-up and restoration. The location is ready — and it stays that way through the shoot.

PHASE 04ARC

The archive — CZ & SK

Twenty-five years of scouting, photographed and catalogued. For any brief I can pull a curated selection within days — happy to share on request.
