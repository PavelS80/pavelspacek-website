01 — About

Pavel Špaček

Twenty-five years. Hundreds of films. One location at a time.

I've been doing this work since 2001. The range of projects has been wide — Czech and Slovak features, Hollywood studio productions, Indian Bollywood films, German and international series. Each one had its own logic, and I've learned something from every single one.

I'm not chasing the biggest names. I'm interested in doing good work on projects that deserve it — whether that's a modest local film or a major international production. I bring the same care regardless of scale.

I'm happy to work with new people. If you're looking for locations across Czech Republic or Slovakia, I can put together a curated selection from our archive — or we can simply start with a conversation. No pressure.
