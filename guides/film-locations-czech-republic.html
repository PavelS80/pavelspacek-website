Locations

Film locations in the Czech Republic

Castles, baroque and nineteenth-century streets, factories, fortresses, villages and open landscape, all within reach of Prague. I have scouted and run these locations for productions since 2001. Send me your brief and you get a first selection from my archive of 25,000+ locations within days, free and with no obligation.

How to get a first selection

  • Send the script or a moodboard and your dates to pavel@filmhunters.cz. One location or a full breakdown, any size of production.
  • Within days you get real options from an archive built over 25 years of scouting across the Czech Republic and Slovakia.
  • Then it is up to you. If you want to go further, I take it on: recces, contracts with owners, permits, on-set location management. There is no obligation.
