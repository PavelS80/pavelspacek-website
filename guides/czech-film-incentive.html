Guide for producers

The Czech film incentive in 2026

The Czech Republic pays a cash rebate on eligible Czech spend: 25% for live-action features, series and documentaries, 35% for animation and digital production, plus 66% of the withholding tax actually paid in the Czech Republic for foreign cast and crew. It is administered by the State Fund for Audiovisual (SFA).

Timeline

  • Applications can be filed at any time of the year; costs can be claimed from up to six months before the application.
  • Production must start within six months of the registration decision, and a further ten shooting days (or all of them, if fewer) must follow within three months.
  • The payout is requested within three years of the registration decision.

What changed in 2026

  • On 27 March 2026 the SFA stopped accepting new registrations because the 2026 allocation was fully committed to earlier projects. Projects already registered continued without restriction.
  • Intake reopened on 1 September 2026. New projects now receive a registration decision without a stated incentive amount; the amount is added when funds become available, and the SFA says the time from registration to a confirmed amount can be up to two years.
  • The SFA has introduced a regular technical shutdown of intake at the end of each calendar year, announced in advance.

For a production this means: register early, budget the rebate as cash-flow that may arrive late, and keep the Czech spend clearly documented from the first scout. Locations, permits and local crew paid in the Czech Republic are Czech expenditure; which items are eligible is defined by the SFA rules. This page is a summary, not tax or legal advice.

Common questions

What is the Czech film incentive rate in 2026?

25% of eligible Czech spend for live-action features, series and documentaries, 35% for animation and digital production, plus 66% of the withholding tax paid in the Czech Republic for foreign cast and crew.

What is the minimum spend for a feature film?

CZK 18 million of Czech spend for a feature of at least 60 minutes.

Is the Czech incentive open for applications?

Intake was suspended on 27 March 2026 and reopened on 1 September 2026. New registrations may be issued without a stated amount, which is added when funds are available; the SFA also plans a short shutdown at the end of each year.
