Why it works

  • Central European architecture in one city. Baroque and historicist Prague reads as Vienna; nineteenth-century apartment blocks and wide boulevards as other Central European capitals; monumental public buildings as Moscow or Berlin.
  • Industrial and military sites a drive from Prague. Ralsko, Milovice, Josefov and Terezín appear repeatedly in productions that need barracks, fortifications or open land.
  • Studios next to the streets. Barrandov sits inside Prague, so a shoot can combine stage work with real locations in the same week.

The right double depends on the period and the shot: what the camera sees, what needs dressing or VFX, and what the permits allow. That is the first conversation I have on every brief.

Common questions

Which cities has Prague doubled for?

Documented examples include Moscow (Child 44, Mission: Impossible – Ghost Protocol), Vienna (Amadeus, The Illusionist, The Gray Man), Warsaw (The Zookeeper's Wife), Berlin (The Gray Man) and wartime Germany (Das Boot).

Where was Child 44 filmed?

In Prague (National Theatre, metro stations, National Museum, Rudolfinum, Barrandov) and in Ralsko, standing in for Moscow. Pavel Špaček was the location manager.
