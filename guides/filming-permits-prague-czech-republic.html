Guide for producers

Filming permits in Prague and the Czech Republic

There is no national film permit office in the Czech Republic. Permits are issued by each municipality and local authority, and on private property you deal directly with the owner. In Prague that means a separate permit from every city district you shoot in, and for most street work also the road administration and the traffic police.

What a Prague street application contains

For special use of a road in Praha 1 the district lists: the application form for occupation for concerts, film and TV production; two site plans; a copy of the trade licence or commercial register extract; a power of attorney if a location company applies for the production; the consent of the road owner; and the opinion of the traffic police. An administrative fee applies under Act No. 634/2004 Coll.

How long it takes

  • A standard administrative procedure has up to 30 days, 60 in complex cases (Section 71 of the Administrative Procedure Code).
  • Praha 1 greenery: apply one month ahead, two months for complex events; processing depends on the district council.
  • Praha 1 also keeps a gap between shoots at the same place, so popular streets can be booked out.

Drones

  • Commercial drone operation is regulated. Flying over populated areas needs a special permission from the Civil Aviation Authority. For operators registered in another EU country this is usually a matter of days; for drones not registered in the Czech Republic it can take several months (Czech Film Commission).
  • Within the Prague airport control zone (CTR LKPR) the limit is 100 m above ground and stricter rules apply within 5.5 km of the airport. Request via the airport's “UAS Flight in CTR LKPR” form (zadosti.uas@prg.aero); the airport answers usually within 5 working days, air navigation services within 3.
  • Consent of the landowner and of people filmed is needed.

Outside Prague

The same logic applies: the town hall for public space, the road authority for roads, the owner for property. Smaller towns are often faster and cheaper, which is one reason productions combine Prague with locations in the regions.

Common questions

Do I need a film permit to shoot in Prague?

Yes for anything that occupies public space: each Prague city district issues its own permit, and street work usually also needs the road administration (TSK) and the traffic police. There is no single national film permit.

How much does it cost to film on Charles Bridge?

Under the Prague filming conditions valid from 1 January 2023, CZK 250,000 per started shooting day plus CZK 100/m² per day for prep and wrap. Confirm current rates before budgeting.

How long do filming permits take in Prague?

Plan for about a month in central Prague. The administrative procedure allows up to 30 days (60 in complex cases), and Praha 1 asks for park applications one to two months ahead.
